Teaching & Service

Teaching Interests

I can teach core undergraduate courses, including introductory programming, object-oriented programming, data structures, algorithms, and software engineering. I have already taught most of these. I would like to teach software maintenance and evolution and software testing and debugging at both the undergraduate and graduate levels. I can also teach machine learning, which I taught at DUET. I plan to develop a graduate seminar on AI for software engineering.
